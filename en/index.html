An early-2000s Indonesian internet cafe, in your browser

Enter the warnet → ▶ Watch the 60-second flashback

In 2000s Indonesia, most people met the internet at a warnet (warung internet): a plywood booth, a CRT monitor, a dolphin wallpaper and a billing timer in the corner. Those cafes are mostly gone. Warnet Nusantara.Net rebuilds one as a web desktop.

What's inside

  • Warnet billing lock screen with dolphins, a voucher on a sticky note and a running timer.
  • Time-travel browser through the Wayback Machine: detik.com 2001, Kaskus, Friendster 2003.
  • Game Warnet: 46 legal games (DOS via the Internet Archive emulator, web ports) and 18 memorials for online games.
  • Real Winamp 2 (Webamp) playing chiptunes generated in your browser.
  • mIRC-style chat with bots in #jakarta #bandung #warnet asking "asl pls".
  • Messenger with BUZZ, Minesweeper-style Ranjau and Nokia-style snake.

Free and open source

No account, no install, works on phones. Zero dependencies and no build step; the code is on GitHub under AGPL-3.0.
