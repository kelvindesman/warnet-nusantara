## When the open licenses are enough

You do **not** need a commercial license to:

- Play the game, link to it, stream it, or make videos about it.
- Fork the code, modify it and host your own copy, as long as you follow the AGPL-3.0 (including publishing the complete corresponding source of your modified version to everyone who uses it over a network) and rename your fork (see [TRADEMARK.md](TRADEMARK.md)).
- Reuse the creative content non-commercially with attribution and under the same license.

## When you need a commercial license

Contact the copyright holder before any of the following:

- **Proprietary or closed-source use**: shipping the code, or a derivative, without releasing your source under the AGPL-3.0.
- **White-label versions**: rebranding the experience for a company, brand campaign, product launch or event.
- **Kiosk and venue installations**: running it on machines in a cafe, museum, exhibition, mall, real warnet or gaming center as part of a paid or promotional service, where you do not want AGPL obligations or need the content under commercial terms.
- **Ad-supported reskins**: any version that carries advertising, sponsorship placements, paywalls or in-app purchases.
- **Commercial use of the creative content**: using the Indonesian UI copy, bot lines, memories/rules texts, icons, dolphin art, chiptune compositions or wallpapers in a commercial product, merchandise or campaign.
- **Use of the name "Warnet Nusantara.Net" or its logo** in anything commercial.

## What a commercial license can include

Terms are negotiated per project and can include: a release from AGPL-3.0 obligations for your build, commercial rights to the creative content, permission to use the name, custom content (your brand on the billing screen, portal banners), and optional support.

Contributions to this project are accepted under the [CLA](CLA.md), which is what allows the copyright holder to offer these terms.

## Contact

Email **`kelvindsmn@gmail.com`** with the subject `Commercial license: <your project>` and describe:

1. Who you are and what you want to build.
2. Where and how it will be deployed (web, kiosk, app, event) and for how long.
3. Whether it is ad-supported, paid, or promotional.
