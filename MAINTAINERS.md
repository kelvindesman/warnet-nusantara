## Roles

| Role | Who | Can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Contributor** | Anyone with a merged pull request | Open issues and PRs, comment, review. |
| **Triager** | Trusted contributors | Label issues (`needs-triage` → `ready-for-agent` / `ready-for-human` / `wontfix`), close duplicates, moderate Discussions, review content PRs. Member of the `@warnet-nusantara/triage` team, which co-owns `data/**`. |
| **Maintainer** | Experienced triagers | Review and merge PRs (after the gate passes), cut releases, help shape the roadmap. Write access to the repository. |
| **Owner** | Kelvin (`@kelvindesman`) | Everything above, plus licensing, commercial agreements, trademark, security advisories, org settings, workflows and final say on disputes. Code owner of `js/`, `css/`, `*.html` and `.github/`. |

## Self-serve ladder

Promotion is mostly automatic to start, and always human to finish. **Write access is never granted automatically.**

1. **Contributor → Triager.** When you reach **3 merged PRs**, a bot opens a *triage nomination* issue for you. If you want the role, comment to accept. The owner approves by adding a single label (`approved`), after which you are added to the triage team.
2. **Triager → Maintainer.** When you reach **10 merged PRs** (and have been triaging for a while), the same bot opens a *maintainer nomination* issue. Same flow: you accept, the owner approves with one label, and the owner grants write access by hand.

The owner may also nominate people directly, or decline a nomination with a short, kind explanation.

## Expectations

- Follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and be welcoming to first-time contributors.
- Never merge a PR whose gate has not passed, and never merge your own PR without a second review once there are at least two maintainers.
- Keep private information (contact details, account handles, operational notes) out of tracked files.

## Stepping down

Life happens. Tell the owner, or simply stop; after 6 months of inactivity, roles move to emeritus status, and you can come back any time.
